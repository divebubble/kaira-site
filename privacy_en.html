Privacy Policy for Kaira

Last updated: November 11, 2026

Kaira (“the app”) is developed by Dominique Mikes, Switzerland.

What data the app processes

Everything you enter in Kaira – your name, your date of birth, moments, photos, people, birthdays, notes and ideas – is stored only locally on your device.

  • Kaira has no user account and no server of its own.
  • No data is transferred to the developer or to third parties.
  • Kaira contains no ads, no analytics and no tracking services.

Photos

When you add a photo to a moment, you choose it with the iOS photo picker. Kaira only receives the selected image and stores a copy locally in the app. Kaira has no access to the rest of your photo library.

Backups

If you have turned on iCloud backups or backups to your computer, the app's data may be part of those backups. They are managed by Apple or by you.

Deleting

You can delete individual entries in the app. When you delete the app, all of its data is removed from the device.

Contact

Questions about privacy: kaira@alderava.ch

Changes

If anything in this policy changes, the current version will be published here.
