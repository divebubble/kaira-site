Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

In short: Kaira does not collect any data about you. Everything you record in the app stays on your iPhone.

Data processed by the app

In Kaira you can record moments (text and photos), people, ideas and birthdays. This data is stored exclusively locally on your device. It is not transmitted to me or to any third party.

Kaira has no user account, no server of its own, no advertising and no tracking. No third-party analytics or advertising services are integrated.

Permissions

Photos

When you add a photo to a moment, you select it in the iOS photo picker. Kaira only receives the images you explicitly select and stores them locally in the app.

Other permissions

Kaira does not request any other permissions, in particular no access to contacts, location, camera or notifications.

Backups

Kaira's data is part of your regular iPhone backup. Whether and where this backup is stored (for example in iCloud) is up to you, in your iPhone settings. Apple's privacy terms apply to that.

This website

This website is hosted with GitHub Pages. When you visit it, GitHub processes technically necessary data such as your IP address in order to deliver the page and protect against abuse. The website itself sets no cookies and uses no analytics services. For more information, see GitHub's privacy statement.

Your rights

Since I do not receive any personal data from the app, I cannot provide information about it or delete it. You are in control of your data at all times: if you delete the app, all entries are removed. For privacy questions, you can reach me at the address above. Swiss data protection law (FADP) and, where applicable, the GDPR apply.

Changes

If Kaira later gains features that process data differently (for example iCloud sync), this policy will be updated beforehand.
