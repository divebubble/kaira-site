Frequently asked questions

Where are my entries stored?

Only locally on your iPhone. Kaira has no server of its own and no user account.

How do I back up my data?

Kaira is included in your regular iPhone backup, either via iCloud Backup or a backup on your computer. When you set up a new iPhone from a backup, your entries are back.

Does Kaira remind me about birthdays and getting in touch?

Yes, but only inside the app: under "This week" and "Loved ones" you can see which birthdays are coming up and whom you wanted to contact again. Kaira does not send notifications.

Kaira cannot see my photos.

When you add a photo to a moment, you select it directly in the photo picker. Kaira only gets access to the images you choose there.

How do I delete all my data?

If you delete Kaira from your iPhone, all entries are removed. Copies may still exist in older iPhone backups.
