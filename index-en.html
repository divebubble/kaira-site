Kaira

traces of what matters

Every dot is a week. The glowing ones are the weeks you want to remember.

Kaira is a journal in weeks. Instead of long entries, you note what made a week matter, with a photo or a single sentence.

Your weeks

A grid shows your time at a glance. Capture moments, link them to the people who were there, and use colored lights and filters to spot what shapes your weeks.

Your loved ones

Collect ideas for the people close to you, see at a glance whom you would like to reach out to again, and keep the next birthdays in view.

Perspective

A life view in blocks of years shows how much time lies behind and ahead of you. "This week" gently brings you back to the present.

Kaira is not a social network. No likes, no followers, no ads. Your entries stay on your iPhone.
