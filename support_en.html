Kaira – Support

Do you have a question about Kaira, found a bug or have an idea? I'd love to hear from you.

Write to kaira@alderava.ch. I usually reply within a few days.

FAQ

Where is my data stored?

Only on your iPhone. Kaira has no account and no server. If iCloud backups are on, your data is included in them.

How do I move my data to a new iPhone?

Restore the new iPhone from an iCloud or computer backup of the old one. Kaira comes along with all its data.

How do I change the language?

In Kaira under Perspective → Settings → Language, or in the iOS Settings under Apps → Kaira → Language. Then close Kaira and open it again.

How do I delete everything?

Delete single moments, people and ideas right in the app. Deleting the app removes all of its data.

How much does Kaira cost?

Kaira is free, with no ads and no tracking.
