Keep what matters: a week, a moment, the people you love. Kaira shows your life in weeks and gently reminds you to make room for what matters.

For iPhone with iOS 17 or later. Free.

Kaira is a quiet journal in weeks. Instead of writing every day, keep what made a week special: a walk, a conversation, the first snow.

THIS WEEK

Each week, Kaira offers a gentle question and shows what is coming up: this week’s moments, people you want to reach out to, and birthdays just around the corner.

YOUR LIFE IN WEEKS

In the year grid, each week with a saved moment glows in the color of its light: Joy, Love, Calm, Growth, Remembrance, or Adventure. The life grid shows all the weeks of your life at a glance. There is room for approximate memories, too, when you only remember the month or year.

YOUR LOVED ONES

Keep the people who matter close. Kaira reminds you of birthdays, shows when you wanted to reach out, and gathers ideas for time together.

PERSPECTIVE

How many summers, weekends, and full moons are still ahead of you? And how often will you see the people you love, if everything stays as it is? Not a calculation, an invitation.

PRIVATE

Everything stays on your iPhone. No account, no ads, no tracking.
